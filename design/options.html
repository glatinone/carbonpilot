What moved

Cloud compute rose 165% in May — the weekend share of usage went from 15% to 60%.

That is 4.1× the previous baseline, and it is a scheduling decision, not a growth story. Cloud is only 2.4% of emissions but 33% of tracked spend — which is exactly why the decision layer, not the accounting layer, is the product. A second signal in September (diesel +57.7%) is a distribution surge, not a trend.
