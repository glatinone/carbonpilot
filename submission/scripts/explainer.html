What it does

One loop, from data to decision.

Measure → Diagnose → Simulate → Act

01 · Ingest

CSV mapped to canonical activities; interpretation confirmed before anything is written.

02 · Diagnose

Robust median/MAD test finds the months that moved, then attributes drivers.

03 · Simulate

Interventions applied to the stored baseline; a constraint search explores combinations.

04 · Act

Ranked on carbon × cost × effort, weighted by factor confidence, tracked to an owner.

The one architectural rule

The language model never produces a carbon number.

The engine computes

Every kilogram of CO₂ comes from a deterministic, unit-tested calculation engine. The model chooses which analyses to run — and explains the result.

Failure mode: “I could not find that.” Never “here is a plausible number.”

Because it is auditable

Every recommendation shows its arithmetic: the quantity, the factor, the factor’s source and year, and the uncertainty.
