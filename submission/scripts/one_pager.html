CarbonPilot

An AI carbon decision engine. It turns fragmented operational data into prioritised, financially-aware decarbonisation actions — and every number it prints is traceable to a calculation engine, never to the language model.

Measure → Diagnose → Simulate → Act

The problem

  • Companies can already measure their carbon. They still cannot decide what to do about it — the data exists, the decision does not.
  • Reporting is a backward view; management asks what changes next, and what it will cost.
  • Carbon and money are decided separately, and nobody can see the trade-off in one place.

The motivating finding

The same 4,304,333 kWh load costs 3,036 tCO₂e on Indonesia’s grid — rank 19 of 20 in JAPAC, coal 69% — and 399 tCO₂e in New Zealand. Location is a decision variable, not a footnote.

  • Ingest. CSV mapped to canonical activities; interpretation confirmed before anything is written.
  • Diagnose. Robust median/MAD test finds the months that moved, then attributes drivers.
  • Simulate. Interventions applied to the stored baseline; a constraint search explores combinations.
  • Act. Ranked on carbon × cost × effort, weighted by factor confidence, tracked to an owner.

One architectural rule: the language model never produces a carbon number.

JAPAC by design

  • Twenty regional grids benchmarked on one documented basis (Ember data, CC BY 4.0) — a public facility, not a slide.
  • Bilingual EN / ID for the mid-market teams reporting platforms under-serve.

Honest boundaries

  • No compliance reporting (CSRD / SB 253 / CDP) yet; curated point factors; linear scenario maths; no multi-tenancy — stated here rather than discovered by a judge.

Carbon reporting tells you where you are. CarbonPilot tells you what to do next.
